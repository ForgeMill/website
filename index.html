WHAT FORGE IS

AI BRINGS THE INTELLIGENCE.
FORGE GOVERNS THE ACTION.

Forge separates what AI can reason about from what it is allowed to do.

INTELLIGENCE

UNDERSTAND + PROPOSE

Models and engineering tools analyze context, reason about objectives and propose bounded work.

AUTHORITY

AUTHORIZE + EXECUTE + PROVE

ForgeGuard authorizes governed actions, TaskRunner owns execution, and Forge validates the resulting state with evidence.

THE GOVERNED LIFECYCLE

FROM INTENT TO ACTION.
WITHOUT LOSING CONTROL.

01

UNDERSTAND

Materialize the requested work and the state it applies to.

02

PROPOSE

AI and engineering tools analyze the objective and propose bounded work.

03

AUTHORIZE

ForgeGuard determines whether that exact governed action is approved.

04

EXECUTE

TaskRunner owns execution after valid authorization.

05

PROVE

Forge validates resulting state and captures evidence of what occurred.

WHY FORGE

CONTROL IS PART OF
THE ARCHITECTURE.

Forge is built around a simple principle: intelligence and authority are different things.

AI AUTHORITY ISOLATION

Models can propose actions without becoming the authority that approves or executes them.

STATE-BOUND APPROVAL

Authorization is tied to the governed action and state instead of an ambiguous approval detached from execution.

EXECUTION OWNERSHIP

TaskRunner owns governed execution after authorization instead of implicitly granting that authority to an AI provider.

VALIDATION + EVIDENCE

Forge verifies resulting state and preserves evidence rather than ending the lifecycle when an AI produces output.
